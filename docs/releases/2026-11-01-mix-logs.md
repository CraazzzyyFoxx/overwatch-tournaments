# Match logs for mixes: release note

A mix match can now carry an Overwatch match log, parsed into the same per-player statistics and MVP places as a
tournament map, and shown only inside the mix.

**Upload.** `POST /api/v1/admin/logs/upload` (`rpc.stats.logs.upload`) takes `custom_game_id` plus exactly one of
`encounter_id` (a match of that mix) or `lobby_index` instead of `tournament_id`:
- `lobby_index` resolves at upload time to the lobby's live match, else its newest recorded one (422 `no_mix_match`
  when it has neither). The lobby room's automatic upload uses it, so a log still in its quiet window when the host
  presses Record lands on the match it belongs to.
- Gate: the mix's host, a co-host or a superuser (`shared.services.mix_hosts.is_mix_host`), or workspace `log.create`.
  The mix's status is not checked: a closed mix still takes logs.
- The job carries `hint_tournament_id = NULL` and the resolved encounter; the object is stored under
  `logs/mix/{custom_game_id}/{hash}`; quota and audit (`entity_type = "custom_game"`) go to the mix's workspace.
- The same bytes uploaded to the same mix again re-point a job that has no `stats.match` (DONE after a mix Undo or
  Cancel cascaded the match away, FAILED, or AWAITING_LINK) at the new match and re-parse it; a PENDING/PROCESSING job
  is left alone. The same bytes under another mix, or across a tournament and a mix, are a 409
  `stats_upload_scope_conflict`.
- `POST /api/v1/admin/logs/{id}/retry` compares the job's and the encounter's tournament NULL-safely and looks the
  encounter up owner-explicitly, so a mix job can be retried within its mix.

A log never decides a mix result and never adds players: tournament-service still refuses mix evidence
(`owner_not_tournament`). Ingest itself was already mix-aware (binding, writer, impact v2 quintiles).

**Reading.**
- `GET /api/v1/matches/{id}` and `…/kill-feed` serve a mix-owned match without the tournament visibility gate
  (mix history is public); its `encounter.tournament`, `stage` and `stage_item` are `null`. `EncounterRead.tournament_id`
  is now nullable.
- `rpc.balancer.custom.match_history` rows add `stats_match_id` and `log_state` (`processing` | `failed` | `null`,
  from the newest job hinted at the match, only while there is no stats match).
- `/api/v1/admin/logs/history` rows add `custom_game_id` and `custom_game_name`; the admin monitor labels them
  «Микс: <name>».
- Every stats reader outside the mix now requires `encounter.tournament_id IS NOT NULL`: player overview, profile hero
  stats, `has_logs`, compare, encounter history, hero records, hero/map stats, and the achievement conditions
  (`global_stat_sum`, `stat_threshold`, `hero`, `log_stat_rank`, `map_coverage`, `match_event`, `kill_feed`).

**Frontend.** The upload dialog (`LogUploadDialog`, was `TournamentLogUploadDialog`) and `useLogAutoUpload` take an
upload scope: tournament, mix match or mix lobby. A lobby room has «Загрузить логи» with automatic upload; each history
row has an upload button, a «Статистика» button opening the tournament match modal (now `MatchStatsDialog`, shared with
the encounter page, without the `/matches/{id}` link for a mix) and a «Лог обрабатывается» / «Лог не привязан» chip.
`workspace.logs` also stales the mix queries.

## Migration and deploy

One migration after `impactv202`: **`mixstatsmv01`**. It recreates `stats.mv_entity_global_stats` (and
`uq_mv_entity_global_stats`) with tournament matches only, so mix games neither feed the global hero averages nor knock
a hero/stat out of `best`. The view is recreated populated: expect the refresh's usual duration under the migration's
lock. Deploy from one SHA: run the migration, then roll stats-service (+ worker), tournament-service,
balancer-service, the gateway (regenerated `schemas.json`) and the frontend.

### Downgrade cost

Downgrade recreates the view with its previous definition, so mix games feed the global hero averages again. No data
is dropped.

## Wire changes

| Change | Kind |
| --- | --- |
| Upload form `custom_game_id`, `lobby_index`; `tournament_id` no longer required when `custom_game_id` is sent | additive |
| Upload: both or neither of `tournament_id` / `custom_game_id`, or a mix without exactly one target → 422; 422 `no_mix_match` | new refusals |
| Log history `custom_game_id`, `custom_game_name` | additive |
| Mix match history `stats_match_id`, `log_state` | additive |
| `GET /matches/{id}`, `…/kill-feed` serve mix matches; `encounter.tournament_id` nullable | changed meaning |
