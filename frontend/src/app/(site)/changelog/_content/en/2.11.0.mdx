### Leagues are tournaments

The league badge and the **League** filter are gone. A league season opens like any other tournament: each day is a branch of its stages, and the season table at the end adds the days up.

League days now count everywhere a tournament does: in profiles, statistics and achievements. Achievements earned before the merge stay.
