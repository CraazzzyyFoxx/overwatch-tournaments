<Lead>Tournament pages move to the new design and show what is happening now. Mixes get lobby codes, the Overwatch MVP is read from the heroes played, and the home page is rebuilt.</Lead>

### Tournament pages

A tournament's overview is built around its current phase: a headline fact, a “now” step, and before the start, the teams and maps. Statistics, participants, matches and the map pool move to the new design too. A stage's format is a chip that opens an animated diagram of how that format plays.

A team's live marker in the bracket opens its stream in a corner of the screen, beside the main broadcast. A participant's POV recording lists where each of their team's matches starts, on the Stream tab and the match page.

### Mixes

The host enters the game's **Lobby code**, and players copy it from the mix page, the lobby room, **Your seat** and the Discord lineup card.

<More href="/docs/players/mixes">Learn more: mixes</More>

### Profile and MVP

The profile's **Heroes** tab sets each stat against the hero's average and draws it as a radar, with the tournament filter right in it. Every best game names its map.

The Overwatch MVP is now read from the heroes played: each stretch on a hero is measured against that hero's averages, and each role's stat weights are fitted on won maps.

<More href="/docs/players/stats">Learn more: statistics</More>

### Home page

The home page is rebuilt: a results feed, a tournament showcase, platform numbers, a podium with records and product tours. A separate page tells organizers what a community gives them, and a tournament without a cover shows its last played map.
