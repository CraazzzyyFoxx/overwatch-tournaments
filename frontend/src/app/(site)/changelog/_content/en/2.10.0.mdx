<Lead>A tournament is now built as a flow: registrations, team formations and stages are steps you connect on one canvas, and a league season is one tournament with a table across its days.</Lead>

### Flow tab

The tournament's admin section has a new **Flow** tab. Drag steps onto the canvas, connect them (blue dots carry players, amber dots carry teams) and press **Save flow**: the checks run while you build, and the graph is saved in one go. **Presets** start you off with a single stage, groups and a playoff, groups with a re-formation of teams before the playoff, an individual Swiss with a draft, or a league season of N days where the best K results count. Each step has its own status and settings in the side panel.

### League table

The public **Table** tab shows the season: a column for each day, the results that count highlighted, and the rule in words above the table ("Only the best 2 results count."). It updates live while the season runs.

### For players

Tournaments have a **Format** tab, a read-only picture of the flow with the status of every step. The **Bracket** tab groups stages by day when a tournament has several. A tournament with several registrations shows a register card per open one, such as **Register for Day 3**.
