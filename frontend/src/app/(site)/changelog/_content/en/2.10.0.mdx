<Lead>A tournament is now built as a flow: registrations, team formations and stages are steps you connect on one canvas. Every league season is one tournament with a table across its days.</Lead>

### Flow tab

The tournament's admin section has a new **Flow** tab. Drag steps onto the canvas, connect them (blue dots carry players, amber dots carry teams) and press **Save flow**: the checks run while you build, and the graph is saved in one go. **Presets** start you off with a single stage, groups and a playoff, groups with a re-formation of teams before the playoff, an individual Swiss with a draft, or a league season of N days where the best K results count. Each step has its own status and settings in the side panel.

### Leagues are tournaments

Every past league season is now one tournament: each day is a branch of its stages, and the season table at the end adds the days up. The league badge and the **League** filter are gone. League days now count everywhere a tournament does: in profiles, statistics and achievements. Achievements earned before the merge stay.

### League table

The public **Table** tab shows the season: a column for each day, the results that count highlighted, and the rule in words above the table ("Only the best 2 results count."). It updates live while the season runs. Search it by player name, sort it by any column, and hover a day for its team, role, wins–draws–losses and Buchholz. A season can rank **by role**: tanks, damage and supports get their own places, and days played in one role never count toward another.

### For players

The tournament overview describes the format in words, step by step: how players sign up, how teams are formed and who advances. The **Bracket** tab groups stages by day when a tournament has several. A tournament with several registrations shows a register card per open one, such as **Register for Day 3**.

#### For organizers

- A new tournament starts from a preset you pick when you create it.
- How teams are formed (balancer, captain draft, registered teams or manual) and the draft format are set on the formation step in the **Flow** tab, not in the tournament settings.
- Changing the flow, a step's settings and moving a step between statuses needs the workspace permission to update tournaments.
- Group tables no longer draw the **advances** cut line, because who goes through is now decided by the filter step after the stage.
