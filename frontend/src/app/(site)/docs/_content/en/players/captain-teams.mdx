Some tournaments are played by ready-made teams: a captain registers the team and recruits the roster by invitation. This page covers founding a team, sending and accepting invites, the requirements a roster has to meet, and what leaving a team costs you.

## When this applies

Only when the organizer chose this way of forming teams. It is easy to spot: the tournament page offers **Register a team** instead of **Register**, and the **Participants** tab carries a **Team applications** section.

There are two ways in: found your own team, or accept a captain's invite. Both spend your single registration for that tournament.

<Callout>
If you already signed up as an individual player you cannot found a team: "You are registered as an individual player, so you cannot create a team — but a captain can still invite you, and accepting will move your existing registration onto their roster." There is no way around it — withdrawing to "free yourself up" does not work, because withdrawal is final.
</Callout>

## Founding a team

1. Press **Register a team** on the tournament page.
2. Enter a **Team name** — it cannot contain "#". If the name is taken the site says "A team with this name is already registered." Add a **Logo** if you like.
3. Pick **Your slot**: "You are a member of the team like anyone else — pick the slot you will play."
4. Fill in the ordinary registration form — the same steps as a solo entry: see [Registration](/docs/players/registration).
5. Press **Create team**. You get "Team created. Invite your players next."

You are now the captain. The roster can be managed by the captain and by any member the captain promotes with **Make manager**.

## Invites

Your team panel lives on the **Participants** tab. **Invite a player** — or **Invite to the … slot** next to a specific open slot — offers two ways:

- **Invite an account** — "The invite goes to the player you pick, and only they can take the slot." You pick from the free agents: players already signed up for the tournament but not yet on a team.
- **Create a link** — "A link is not reserved for an account: whoever joins with it first takes the slot." Its lifetime is set by **Link valid for**.

<Callout type="warning">
A link is shown exactly once: "It is shown once and cannot be recovered. Anyone with the link can take this slot." Copy it immediately with **Copy link**.
</Callout>

An open invite **holds the slot**: you cannot issue a second one for it — "That slot already has an open invite — revoke it first." **Revoke** frees it again. An expired link can be given more time with **Extend**, or replaced with **New link**, which kills the old one.

A team also has an overall invite ceiling counting every invite it ever issued, revoked ones included: "This team has issued too many invites. Withdraw an outstanding one, or ask an organizer to reset the count." Where they all went is listed under **Invite history**, with states **Open**, **Accepted**, **Declined**, **Withdrawn** and **Expired**.

## Starters and substitutes

Starters are the tournament's roster slots (**Tank**, **Damage**, **Support** and how many of each). The roster counts as complete once every starter slot is filled; until then the panel says "Still needed: …".

Substitutes are a separate budget, shown as "1 of 2 substitutes". Use **Invite a substitute** to recruit one. Members can be moved between the starting line-up and the bench with **To bench** and **To start**. An open substitute invite likewise holds a bench place until it is answered.

<Callout>
A complete roster is not an entry: "A complete roster does not mean tournament entry: the organizer adds the team." The organizer sets each team's admission separately — **Pending**, **Accepted** or **Waitlisted** — and only complete, accepted teams are added to the tournament.
</Callout>

The captain can freeze the roster themselves with **Lock roster**, which is only available on a complete roster. After that nothing can change: "The captain locked this roster. Ask an organizer to unlock it to make changes."

## Accepting an invite

On the site:

- An **account invite** shows up under **Team invites** on the **Participants** tab of that tournament: "… invited you to play …", with **Join team** and **Decline**.
- A **link invite** opens the **Team invite** page. The team, the tournament and the offered slot are visible before you even sign in. Then **Sign in** and **Join the team**.

In Discord: the "Team invite" direct message arrives with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons.

What happens next depends on whether you already have an entry for that tournament:

- **No entry yet** — you fill in the registration form as you join. The slot is already decided: "Your slot on this team is … and cannot be changed here."
- **You already registered** — that entry is simply moved onto the roster. You do not fill the form in again; your roles, ranks and answers stand. Only the slot changes, and the captain picked it.

Declining is one press of **Decline**: "Decline this invite? The captain will be able to offer the slot to someone else."

## Roster requirements

The organizer can set rules a team must satisfy. Only starters are checked — substitutes fall outside the rank limits.

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A starter has no rank for this roster. | The tournament needs a rank and this player has none. |
| A starter is below the minimum rank. | A minimum rank is set for starters. |
| A starter is above the maximum rank. | A maximum rank is set for starters. |
| Starter ranks are too far apart. | There is a cap on the gap between the strongest and weakest starter. |
| A player has no linked Discord account. | The tournament requires Discord server membership and the account is not linked. |
| A player is not in the required Discord server. | Join the community server. |
| Discord membership could not be verified. | The check did not run. The roster is still held back — try later or contact the organizer. |
| Discord membership is required but no server is bound. | Something for the organizer to fix. |
| A Discord identity on this roster is already on another team. | The same player is counted on another team in this tournament. |

## Invite errors

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| This invite has expired — ask the captain for a new one. | The captain can **Extend** it or issue a new link. |
| The captain withdrew this invite. | The link no longer works. |
| This invite was already declined. | Ask the captain for a new link. |
| Someone already took this slot. | Another player joined with the link first. |
| This invite was sent to a different account. | It is an account invite. Sign in with that account, or ask for a link. |
| Your registration for this tournament is no longer active, so you cannot take a team slot. | An invite does not revive a withdrawn or rejected entry. |
| That player already joined a team. Pick someone else. | The free agent was recruited before you. |
| That player has no site account. Send them a link instead. | Account invites only work for existing accounts. |
| Too many attempts. Wait a little and try again. | Wait a minute and retry. |

## Leaving, removing, disbanding

<Callout type="warning">
Every way out of a team withdraws your registration, and withdrawal is final — you cannot return to that tournament, not even on a fresh invite.
</Callout>

- **Leave the team** is your own call: "Leave this team? Your registration is withdrawn."
- A captain cannot leave: "Transfer captaincy or disband the team instead of leaving." Hand it over with **Make captain** ("Hand captaincy to …? You will no longer be able to edit the roster."). Only a starter can be made captain.
- **Remove from team** is for the captain or a manager: "Remove … from the team? Their registration is withdrawn and the slot reopens."
- **Disband the team** — "Disband the team? Every member's registration is withdrawn. This cannot be undone."

## When the roster stops changing

The roster is editable while the team is still forming and registration is open. Otherwise the panel names the reason outright:

| Message | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| "Registration is closed — the roster can no longer change." | The tournament's registration window ended. |
| "The captain locked this roster." | Only an organizer can unlock it. |
| "This team is in the tournament — the organizer froze its roster." | The team was added to the tournament. |
| "An organizer rejected this team — the roster can no longer change." | The organizer writes the reason beside it. |
| "This team is disbanded — the roster can no longer change." | The team no longer exists. |

When a team is rejected, the organizer decides whether the members' registrations go with it. If they kept them alive, you can still accept an invite from another team.
