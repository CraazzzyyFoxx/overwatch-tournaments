A scrim is a practice match between two teams with a full pre-game but no tournament around it. You open a room, send the link to the other captain, and play the series under the same veto rules an official match uses.

## What you need first

- **An account.** "A room needs two captains, and a captain needs an account."
- **A selected workspace.** Scrim rooms belong to a workspace, so pick one in the site header to see yours.

The page is called **Scrims** and opens from the site menu.

## Creating a room

1. Press **New room**.
2. Set a **Room name** — both captains see it at the top of the room.
3. Fill in the **Home team**. That side is yours: "You captain this side."
4. Fill in the **Away team**. The first person to open the link claims it.
5. Choose the **Series length**. It sets the veto order too.
6. Set the rules in one of two ways:
   - **Copy a round** — reuse the rules of a tournament, stage or round you already play;
   - **Author it** — pick a **Map pool**, optionally turn on **Ban heroes too** with its own **Hero pool** and **Bans per side**, and set a **Turn timer** (leave it empty for no time limit).
7. Press **Create room**.

## Inviting your opponent

Every row in the room list has a **Copy link** action. Send that link to the other captain — the link is the invitation.

When they open it, the room offers them the free side: "This room still needs a captain for…", with **Captain** and **Just watch** buttons. Whoever presses first takes the side, and after that no side is left to claim.

You can share the link with teammates too — they open the room as spectators: "Only the two captains veto. Everyone else can follow along." Until the opponent joins, the room is marked **Waiting for the opponent**.

## How a scrim runs

From here it works exactly like a tournament match: **Map veto**, then **Hero bans** for the chosen map if they are enabled, then you play the map and both captains report the score. Matching reports lock the map in and open the next one; different reports count as a dispute.

One thing is different: there is no series-wide **Match Report** here. A scrim publishes no official result, so only the individual map results are filed.

Everything else about the pre-game — the steps, who goes first, undo, the room chat — is covered in [Matches: pick-ban and results](/docs/players/matches).

## What a scrim does not affect

Nothing at all. "Scrims stay between the two teams: they never show up in encounters, statistics or tournament listings." A scrim result never reaches a standings table, never changes your profile and is not visible to outsiders.

Only members of the same workspace can open the room — for everybody else it simply does not exist.

## How many rooms you can keep open

One creator can only keep a limited number of rooms open at the same time. By default that is one room: close the previous one to create the next. The limit is configurable, so a busy community may allow more.

## Closing a room

The room list has a **Close room** action. The creator or the captain of either side can close it.

Closing frees the slot for a new room and marks the old one **Closed**. Nothing is deleted: scrim history is kept, and the participants can still open it later.

<Callout>
A closed room accepts no further actions. If the series is not finished, it is too early to close — play it out first.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page asks you to sign in | A scrim is run by two captains, so it needs an account |
| "No workspace selected" | Pick a workspace in the site header |
| You cannot create a second room | Close the first one — the number of open rooms is limited |
| Your opponent opened the link and got nothing | They have to be a member of the same workspace |
| The side is already taken | Somebody else claimed it — sides go to whoever presses first |
