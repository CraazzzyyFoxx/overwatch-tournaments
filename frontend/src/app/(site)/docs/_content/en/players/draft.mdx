A draft is an open team-building session: captains take turns picking players out of one shared pool, and everyone else watches it happen live. This page explains how the draft room works and what happens when you get picked.

## When a tournament has a draft

A draft only runs where the organizer chose that way of forming teams. In other tournaments the balancer builds the rosters instead, and there is no draft room at all.

The **Draft** button appears on the tournament page once the tournament reaches check-in — before that the room does not exist yet. The link opens the public draft board, which anyone can watch without signing in.

## Who does what

- **Captains** pick players. A pick can be prepared ahead of your turn: click a player to open their card with roles, stats and the **Pick** button. The button works only on your turn.
- **The organizer** starts, pauses and resumes the draft, adds time, fires the autopick, rolls the last pick back and can override a captain's pick — with a mandatory reason that goes into the **Journal**.
- **Everyone else** watches. The board updates live; a spectator sees the whole pool and every player card, but no picking controls.

## What is on screen

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Clock strip** | The timer, the team on the clock and its captain, the pick and round number, the picks of the current round, the last pick and who picks next |
| **Players** | The pool: **Available**, **My list** (captains only) and **All** tabs, role filters, search and a **Fit** column — how well a player suits the team you pick for. Spectators get a **Demand** column instead: how many teams can still take the player |
| **Teams** | **Rosters** (each team's slots and average rating; you can **follow** teams), **Queue** (the next picks and the history) and **Order** (every round as a table) |
| **Player card** | Roles in priority order with rank, sub-role and heroes, tournament and hero stats, recent tournaments and the registration answers |
| **Room chat** | The shared chat; captains and organizers write, everyone else reads |

## Pick order

The captain order and its format are set by the organizer before the draft starts. There are three formats:

- **Snake** — the direction reverses after every round. Whoever picked last in one round picks first in the next.
- **Linear** — the same captain order is used every round.
- **Custom** — the organizer sets a separate rule per round: in order, in reverse, weakest or strongest captain first, lowest or highest team average first.

Rules based on a team's average rating work off the live rosters, so that round's order is only known when the round itself begins, not in advance.

## Timer, overtime and autopick

Every pick has a **Pick time** — the countdown a captain sees on their turn. If the organizer enabled **Overtime**, the captain gets a few extra seconds after the main time runs out, and only then does the turn close automatically.

When the clock is gone, the platform makes the pick instead of the captain, and the pick is marked with the **Auto** badge. If the captain has a **My list**, the autopick takes the first player from it who can still be picked — the list order is the priority. With an empty list the autopick takes the best player for an open role. While it is their turn, the captain sees in the pick hint exactly whom the autopick would take.

An autopick never breaks the draft: it only takes a player and a role that still leave every remaining team able to fill its positions. The same rule applies to manual picks — an unavailable role in the pool is drawn dashed, and its hint says why, for example "Choosing this option would leave another required role impossible to fill."

## Why a draft pauses

The **Paused** state means picking is temporarily closed. That happens for three reasons:

1. **The organizer paused it by hand** — for example, to wait for a captain.
2. **A role shortage.** If no option is left that still lets every team build a legal roster, the draft stops itself instead of making a pick that would strand somebody. The organizer edits the roles of players in the pool and resumes the draft by hand.
3. **The round order was recalculated.** In formats where the queue depends on team averages, the order is recomputed when the round opens, the draft pauses, and the organizer confirms the new order.

In every case a banner above the board explains why, and captains cannot pick until the draft is resumed.

## What a picked player sees

As an ordinary participant you never have a turn — you get picked. The moment that happens:

- you disappear from the **Available** tab and stay in the **All** tab with your team next to you;
- your card shows the **Drafted** status with the team name and your role;
- you appear under **Teams → Rosters** on your role.

When the draft finishes, a summary appears above the player list (the rating spread between teams, players off their role, autopicks and overrides), and the board shows the final rosters and every recorded pick. Those teams become ordinary tournament teams: they appear in the **Teams** section of the tournament page, and that is the team you play your matches for.

<Callout>
The roles you declared in your registration feed straight into the draft: a captain sees them on your card, and they decide which position you can be taken on. If they are not accurate, fix your registration before it closes — see [Registration](/docs/players/registration).
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Draft** button on the tournament page | Either this tournament uses the balancer, or it has not reached check-in yet |
| You see "Draft is not configured yet" | The organizer has not created and validated the draft; come back later |
| You are a captain but the **Pick** button is missing | Make sure you are signed in with the account registered as captain. The button is active only on your team's turn — before that you can only prepare the pick |
| The draft has been paused for a long time | Only the organizer can resume it — ask them in the room chat or in your community |
