Short answers to the things players trip over most often. If your question is not here, follow the link on the closest answer — the full detail lives in that article.

## Account and sign-in

### How do I switch the site language?

The header has an **RU / EN** switch. When you are signed in it sits in your account menu, next to **Logout**.

### I cannot find the tournament somebody told me about

Every community runs its own site with its own tournaments. Make sure you are on the right one: **Switch workspace** in the header lists the communities you can reach. If the organizer has not published the tournament yet, the site answers **Tournament not found** even on a direct link — see [How a tournament works](/docs/players/tournaments).

### The Discord bot says my Discord is not linked

Direct messages only go to a Discord account linked through OAuth. Open **Account settings → Profile → Linked accounts** and link Discord. If Discord is linked and the bot is still silent, your direct messages are closed or you share no server with the bot: delivery simply does not happen, nothing is queued and nothing is retried. Everything that concerns you personally still lands in your inbox on the site — see [Notifications and the Discord bot](/docs/players/notifications).

### The bot messages me too often

Open **Account settings → Notifications → Discord direct messages** and switch off the groups you do not want: **Tournaments**, **Matches**, **Teams**. Those toggles only decide what Discord mirrors; the inbox on the site keeps everything — see [Notifications and the Discord bot](/docs/players/notifications).

### I have two accounts on the site

One account can own only one player profile. If you created a second account by accident, sign in to the spare one and delete it under **Account settings → Security → Account deletion**: your tournament history stays on the player profile, and the freed Discord and Battle.net connections can then be linked to your main account.

### Somebody else's account owns my player history

The site picks up an existing player profile only when nobody owns it and your OAuth-verified handle matches the one on that profile. If the profile already belongs to another account, you cannot fix it yourself — ask the community organizers. More on linking in [Account and sign-in](/docs/players/account).

## Registration

### The button is dead and says "Registration closed"

Sign-ups open and close on the schedule the organizer set, and it does not depend on the phase the tournament is in right now. If the organizer allows late sign-ups, the form stays open and your application is marked as a late one — which changes nothing else about it. See [Registration](/docs/players/registration).

### The site asks me to make my Overwatch career profile public

When the organizer turned this requirement on, it is checked at check-in: a private profile means you are not admitted. Your participant card shows **Make your Overwatch career profile public**, or **Add your BattleTag** / **Overwatch profile not found — check the BattleTag** when the tag is missing or mistyped. See [Entry requirements](/docs/players/admission).

### It says I need an active subscription

The organizer decides where this is checked: at check-in only, or at sign-up as well. The usual reasons are **No active subscription**, **Link your Discord account**, **Join the Discord server** and **No subscription code entered**. If your subscription is proven by a code from a subscriber-only post, paste it into **Code from the subscriber-only post** and press **Redeem** — see [Entry requirements](/docs/players/admission).

### I withdrew my registration by mistake

<Callout type="warning">
Withdrawing is final: you cannot register for that tournament again, and a team invite will not revive a withdrawn application. If it was a mistake, the only way back is to ask the organizer.
</Callout>

The **Withdraw** button also disappears once you check in: from that point rosters are built from the confirmed attendee list. See [Registration](/docs/players/registration).

### The invite link says "This invite has expired"

An invite link is valid for a limited time set by the captain. Ask them for a new one: their list of open invites has **Extend** and **New link**. A link is shown once and cannot be recovered — see [Captain teams and invites](/docs/players/captain-teams).

### I registered but ended up on no team

Usually one of three things: you did not check in, you have no rank on the role you need (your registration card then says **Waiting for balancing — organizers will assign your rank**), or there were not enough players for one more full team — the balancer only builds complete rosters. What to do next is covered in [Check-in](/docs/players/check-in) and [Draft](/docs/players/draft).

## Check-in

### I missed check-in

Message the organizer. They can check you in by hand at any point, including after the check-in window has closed. See [Check-in](/docs/players/check-in).

### There is no check-in button even though check-in is open

The button only appears when your registration is **Approved**, you have not checked in yet, and the check-in window is currently running. If the status is **Pending review**, wait for the organizer's decision. See [Check-in](/docs/players/check-in).

## Matches

### The match result is wrong

Once a result is confirmed, the site says **This match result has been confirmed and can only be changed by an admin**. Ask the organizer: changing a confirmed result is their action. See [Matches: pick-ban and results](/docs/players/matches).

### My opponent and I reported different results

Then the site shows **Reports differ — result disputed** and the organizers get a notification saying the report on your match is disputed. The organizer decides from there — see [Matches: pick-ban and results](/docs/players/matches).

### We banned the wrong map in pick-ban

A move can be taken back, but only by both sides: press **Ask to undo** and the opponent presses **Agree**. An organizer can undo a move without that agreement. See [Matches: pick-ban and results](/docs/players/matches).

## Stats and ranks

### My rank on the site does not match my rank in game

For a tournament the site takes the rank written on your registration first, then the community's shared ranks, and only then your Overwatch rank from the linked BattleTag. So the rank you play under is the one the organizer decided — that is who to talk to. See [Profile, stats and ranks](/docs/players/stats).

### The match was played but there are no stats for it

Statistics come from the match log files the organizer uploads. Until those are uploaded and parsed the site says **No game logs were parsed for this series, so per-player statistics are unavailable** — the series result itself is still counted normally.

### I did not get an achievement

Achievements are computed from match data, so without uploaded logs they will not appear. Some achievements are granted or revoked by the organizer by hand. See [Achievements](/docs/players/achievements).
