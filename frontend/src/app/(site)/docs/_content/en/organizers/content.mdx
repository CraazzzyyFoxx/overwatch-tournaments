Two admin sections decide what the platform fills its statistics with: the catalogues of heroes, maps and gamemodes, and the background collection of ranks, subscriptions and streams. This page covers what is in each and what you do there.

## Game content

**Admin → Game content** holds the heroes, maps and gamemodes that log parsing and analytics rely on. These catalogues are shared by the whole platform, so the section is open to platform administrators only. It has four pages: **Heroes**, **Maps**, **Gamemodes** and **Unresolved names**.

### Heroes

The hero list: icon, name and role. **Create hero**, **Edit hero** and **Delete hero**. A hero carries a name, an icon link, a role, and the colour it is highlighted with in statistics.

### Maps

The map list: image, name, gamemode and **Mode Pool** — **Competitive** or **Casual**. **Create map**, **Edit map** and **Delete map**. Every map picks a gamemode.

### Gamemodes

The gamemode list, with **Create gamemode**, **Edit gamemode** and **Delete gamemode**.

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting a hero, map or gamemode cannot be undone. A gamemode will not delete while any map still uses it — reassign those maps first.
</Callout>

### Unresolved names

This is where names from match logs land when they match no hero, map or gamemode: a different client language, a typo, a new name after a game update. Until a name is resolved, the statistics line behind it is not counted.

The table shows **Type** (**Hero**, **Map** or **Gamemode**), **Raw name** exactly as it appeared in the log, **Times seen**, **Last seen**, and **Last log** — a link to the log it last appeared in. The filters above are **Type** and **Include resolved**.

To resolve a row:

1. In the **Attach to** column pick the hero, map or gamemode the name means.
2. Press **Attach**. The name becomes an alias of that entry, and later logs carrying it resolve on their own.
3. If the name means nothing useful, press **Dismiss** and the row leaves the queue.

Where the logs come from is covered in [Match logs](/docs/organizers/logs).

## Collectors

**Admin → Collectors** shows whether background collection is working. Three pages: **Rank**, **Subscriptions** and **Streams**. Each has a **Status** tab; the **Settings** tab with the collection parameters is open to platform administrators only, as are the pause controls.

Collector health is one word: **Healthy**, **Degraded**, **Failing** or **Paused**.

### Rank

Collects each game's own ranks through its linked accounts: Overwatch profiles through BattleTags (from OverFast), League of Legends Solo/Duo and Flex through Riot IDs (from the Riot API), Deadlock ranks through Steam accounts (from deadlock-api.com). The game switch at the top of the page picks which game's collector you are looking at. These are the ranks used when a player has none on their entry and none in the workspace book — see [Ranks and divisions](/docs/organizers/divisions).

The page shows **Coverage (snapshots)**, **Fetches (24h)**, **Upstream**, **Accounts** and **Auto-disabled accounts** — accounts whose collection switched itself off after repeated failures. **Re-enable all accounts** turns them back on; for Overwatch it is worth confirming afterwards that the player's profile really is public. When the upstream refuses every fetch, or nothing has been collected for a while although collection is on, a **Rank collection is down** banner appears.

Below that is **Task history**: time, account, status, what triggered the run, the number of snapshots and the error text.

To recheck one player, open their card in **Admin → People**. It shows their current ranks, a table of accounts with status, last check, last success and consecutive failures, plus **Collect all**, **Collect selected** and a per-row **Collect**. If a player has no account for that game there is nothing to collect — ask them to connect Battle.net (Overwatch), Riot (League of Legends) or Steam (Deadlock).

### Subscriptions

Checks the subscriptions of entrants in tournaments that enforce the subscription requirement.

The page shows **Entitlements**, **Players tracked**, **Coverage (checked)** and **Checks (24h)**. **Check all now** runs a check across every tournament that enforces the requirement.

Below that is **Check history** with **Time**, **Player**, **Provider**, **State**, **Tier**, **Trigger** and **Reason** columns. **Reason** is the same message the player sees; what each one means and how to fix it is in [Subscriptions and admission](/docs/organizers/subscriptions).

The providers themselves and the subscription rule are configured elsewhere, in **Admin → Settings → Subscriptions**.

### Streams

Tracks who is live on Twitch right now, so streams can appear on tournament pages, and remembers participants' POV broadcasts, so their recordings get match timestamps. The poll is platform-wide, so the page is not open to every organizer and carries no workspace split.

It shows **Last tick**, **Tournaments**, **Channels polled**, **Live now** and **Rate limit left**. There is no check history here: the page keeps no per-channel log.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Hero or map numbers in statistics do not add up | Check **Unresolved names** — some log lines most likely did not resolve. |
| A player's rank never arrives | Open their card in **Admin → People** and look at the account status; if collection is switched off, re-enable the accounts in **Collectors → Rank**. |
| The subscription check blocks nobody | The provider is most likely half-configured — see [Subscriptions and admission](/docs/organizers/subscriptions). |
| Streams do not show on a tournament page | Look at **Collectors → Streams**: if the poll is paused or failing, the tournament is not the problem. |
| A participant's recording has no match timestamps | A map's timestamp comes from its match log (from the time in the log's file name when the log itself has no time zone) or from the pre-game room, where the bans before the map end. Without them, from the start of the pre-game phase, and without a room, from the match start set in the admin. The streamer must also keep past broadcasts on Twitch. If the timestamps are off, check the POV stream delay in the tournament's **Links** settings. |
