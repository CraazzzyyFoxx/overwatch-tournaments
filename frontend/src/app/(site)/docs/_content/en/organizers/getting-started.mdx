This is the organizer's starting point: how to get a workspace, what it gives you, and how the admin panel is laid out. If you already have a workspace, jump to the recommended setup order.

## Getting a workspace

1. Open the **How to get your own workspace** page — the communities list on the home page links to it.
2. Press **Create workspace**. If you are not signed in yet, the sign-in dialog opens first.
3. Fill in **Slug** — the address, in lowercase Latin letters, digits, hyphen and underscore — and **Name**. **Description** and **Icon** are optional; the icon accepts PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 2 MB.
4. Confirm. You become the owner and administrator of the new workspace and land straight in its settings.

One account can own one workspace. If workspace creation is turned off for your account, the button is disabled and a note explains it — contact the platform team in that case.

A new workspace works immediately: you can run tournaments, invite members and share its link. It appears in the public communities list on the home page only after the platform team reviews it.

## What a workspace gives you

- **Your own address** — a platform subdomain, or a custom domain you connect yourself.
- **Your own look** — a dedicated color theme for the community's public site.
- **Separate content** — your tournaments and stats stay isolated from every other workspace.

## The admin panel at a glance

The panel opens from the site menu — the **Admin** item. Navigation sits on the left, split into groups. Here is what lives in each section.

- **Dashboard** — an operations overview: active tournaments, the issue queue and the actions that need you first.
- **Tournaments** — tournaments, their stages and schedules.
- **People** — the workspace's player identities, the handles linked to them, and the accounts that sign in as them.
- **Teams** — team rosters, per tournament.
- **Matches** — encounters, standings, captain reports, parsed maps and logs, switched with the **Encounters**, **Standings**, **Reports**, **Parsed maps** and **Logs** tabs.
- **Achievements** — achievements and the conditions that earn them.
- **Settings** — every workspace setting, from the name to subscriptions.
- **Members** — who has access to the workspace, and with which role.
- **Collectors** — the health of rank, subscription and stream collection.
- **Access** — roles, permissions, integration keys, connected accounts and sessions.
- **Workspaces** — the workspaces you can reach.
- **Announcements** — notices for this workspace.
- **Audit log** — who changed what, and when.
- **Notifications** — the system notifications this workspace's activity produced.

The menu only shows what your role can reach, so an owner and a host see different lists, and some sections are reserved for the platform team. To jump between sections, use the **Search…** button above the menu or press Ctrl+K.

## Switching workspaces

If you belong to several communities, the workspace switcher (**Switch workspace**) changes the current one — and every admin screen starts showing that workspace: its tournaments, its members, its settings. Check which workspace is selected before you change anything.

## Unsaved changes

Settings do not save themselves. The moment you edit something, an **Unsaved changes** bar appears at the bottom of the page with **Save changes** and **Discard**. Trying to leave the page without saving asks "Discard unsaved changes?" — confirming loses the edits.

A few things work differently and apply immediately: the workspace icon (it uploads as soon as you pick the file), connecting a domain and linking a Discord server. Those are separate steps with their own buttons.

## Recommended setup order

1. **Settings → General** — name, description, timezone and icon. The timezone matters: tournament schedules are entered and shown in it.
2. **Settings → Branding** and **Settings → Visibility** — how the public site looks, and whether outsiders can find the workspace.
3. **Settings → Domain** — the subdomain and, if you want one, a custom domain.
4. **Settings → Discord** and **Settings → Notifications** — the community's server and the channel where the bot announces tournament events.
5. **Settings → Divisions** — the rank bands players are sorted into; see [Divisions](/docs/organizers/divisions).
6. **Settings → Player statuses** and **Settings → Sub-roles** — the statuses and hero sub-roles every tournament in the workspace shares.
7. **Settings → Registration forms** — the registration questionnaires; see [Registration](/docs/organizers/registration).
8. **Members** — hand roles to your organizing team; see [Roles and permissions](/docs/organizers/roles).
9. Create your first tournament — walked through step by step in [Your first tournament](/docs/organizers/first-tournament).

Every workspace settings page is covered in [Workspace settings](/docs/organizers/workspace).
