A stage is one part of a tournament: a group phase, a Swiss round, or a playoff bracket. This page covers building stages, generating the bracket, and setting seeding, points and tiebreakers.

It all lives in the admin: open **Tournaments**, pick the tournament and go to the **Bracket** tab. The stage list is on the left; the editor for the selected stage is on the right, with the sections **General**, **Seeding**, **Tiebreakers**, **Best-of**, **Round schedule** and **Items**.

## Stage formats

**Add stage** creates a stage: **Stage name**, **Stage type**, and a few extra fields for some formats.

| Format | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Round Robin** | Every team in the group plays every other. Nothing advances through a bracket — the Standings are the result. With an odd number of teams, one team sits out each round. |
| **Swiss** | Each round pairs teams on similar results. **Swiss max rounds** sets how many rounds are played. |
| **Single Elimination** | Lose once and you are out. When the field is not a power of two, some teams skip the first round. |
| **Double Elimination** | An upper and a lower bracket; a team is out after its second loss. |

Swiss avoids repeating a pairing that has already been played, and it does not give a second bye to a team that already had one. If the field cannot be paired at all, generation stops with an error — add or remove a team.

<Callout>
Once a stage exists, only platform administrators can change its format: **Only superusers can modify stage type after creation.**
</Callout>

## The life of a stage

The stage header shows its status.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | No matches generated yet. |
| **Preview** | The bracket is generated but the stage is not active. Organizers see it; captains do not. |
| **Active** | The stage is live: matches can be played and reported. |
| **Completed** | The stage is finished. |

**Generate bracket** builds the bracket without activating the stage — that is what preview is for. Until the stage is activated, captains can neither run the map veto nor file a report: their room shows **Bracket preview** and a note saying it opens once you activate the stage.

The usual order is:

1. Create the stage and wire teams into it (the **Items** section).
2. Press **Generate bracket** and check the result in **Bracket preview**.
3. When it looks right, choose **Activate stage** — or **Activate & generate** to do both at once for a playoff bracket.

Once a stage has matches the button reads **Regenerate**. It leaves played matches alone: in a grouped stage only groups with no matches get a new bracket, an all-empty bracket simply gets its seeded teams written in, and anything else is blocked until you delete the existing matches.

**Revert to draft** puts the stage back into draft, but only while none of its matches has started.

<Callout type="warning">
**Delete stage** removes the stage structure, its team slots and its generated matches. **Delete structure item** also removes that group's standings. Neither can be undone.
</Callout>

## Parallel stages

The **Phase** field in **General** sets the wave. Stages sharing a number run in parallel — that is how a High and a Low division run side by side. A higher number is a later wave, fed by the ones before it.

If you activate a stage while an earlier one still has open encounters, the platform warns you first: the seeds freeze against standings that can still change.

## Seeding

The **Seeding** section.

- **Bracket seeds** decides who is seed 1 in the generated bracket: **Slot order (manual / standings)**, **Highest team avg SR first**, **Highest team total SR first** or **Random (stable per stage)**.
- **Teams advancing to playoff (per group)** is how many teams leave each group. Leave it empty to derive the number from the bracket wiring. **Advance count per group** below overrides it for a single group.
- **Wire seeds from** plus the **Wire** button names the group stage that feeds this bracket. It is required when two divisions run in parallel.
- The **Seed by SR** menu item spreads teams across the groups with a snake SR draft. Every manual assignment in the stage is cleared first.
- **Auto-wire from groups** links the playoff to the group stage for you.

### Lower-bracket seeds in double elimination

**Group seeding** decides where the advancing teams land:

- **All advancing → Upper bracket** — everyone starts in the upper bracket.
- **Split: half Upper, half Lower** — half of them start in the lower bracket.

### The grand final

The **Grand final** field in **General**:

- **No reset** — the tournament ends at the grand final.
- **With reset** — if the lower-bracket team wins the grand final, an extra **Grand Final Reset** match is created. It appears only in that case: if the upper-bracket team wins, the grand final is the last match.

## Points and tiebreakers

Points for a result are set on the tournament: **Settings → Scoring**, fields **Win**, **Draw**, **Loss**. A single stage can override them in **Tiebreakers** — **Win points override**, **Draw points override**, **Loss points override**. An empty field means "same as the tournament".

For Swiss, the same section has **Swiss bye points**: what a team scores for a round it sits out. It defaults to the win points.

**Standings preset** picks a ready-made set of tiebreakers (**System default (based on type)**, **Challonge Swiss (Buchholz first)**, **Challonge Round Robin**, **Default bracket**), and the **Tiebreaker evaluation order** list below lets you reorder the steps with the arrows or switch off the ones you do not want — a metric that is switched off is simply not evaluated.

The metrics are **Points**, **Head-to-Head**, **Median Buchholz**, **Buchholz**, **Match Wins** and **Score Differential**. **Points** is the ranking metric: it is always first and cannot be switched off. **Manual Override** is a system step and always comes last.

**Reset to preset defaults** restores the order the chosen preset dictates.

### Manual positions

When teams come out level on every tiebreaker, the **Unresolved ties** panel appears on **Matches → Standings**. Order the teams with the arrows and press **Save order** — that order is stored and survives a recalculation. **Clear override** removes it again.

<Callout type="warning">
**Recalculate** rebuilds every standings row of the tournament from encounter results. Manual edits to individual rows are overwritten — save an order through **Unresolved ties** instead.
</Callout>

Standings count closed rounds only: while a round still has an unplayed encounter, it does not enter the table.

## Series length

In **Best-of**, the **Default** field sets the series length, **Final** (in double elimination, **Grand Final**) sets it for the deciding match, and the rows below set it per round. In double elimination the upper and lower bracket rounds are configured separately.

The length is baked into matches when the bracket is generated. **Apply to existing matches** writes it into matches that already exist, without regenerating.

## How teams advance

- Only a completed match with a decided winner moves a team on.
- A draw cannot finish an elimination match. Try to record a level score and the platform refuses: **An elimination bracket needs a winner — a draw cannot be recorded here.**
- Change the winner of a match that has already been played and every result that depended on it is reset, so those matches have to be played again. See [Running matches](/docs/organizers/matches).
